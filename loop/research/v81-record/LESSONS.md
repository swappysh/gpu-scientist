## 2026-11-15 v8.1 post-mortem (director meta round 5, "healing"): b06b208 "runs at their pace" lost to v7 on held-out late-October data
- What changed: a line with 2+ moves one way in 7 days and none back gets its median pushed by (run's net log change) x h / days since its first move, times the market's share of runs that went on (up to 0.9).
- Where it hurt: on dev, origins Oct 1-7 fell from +0.157 (19a5148) to +0.027. September rose from +0.253 to +0.266. July-August was flat (-0.002).
- It moved 462 of 2774 medians. The 413 small shifts (under 4%) gained a little. The 49 big shifts (over 4%) cost 1.9 in loss (skill on those cells +0.16 to -1.19). 33 of the 49 were Oct 1-7.
- Two lines paid most of it: fastgpu B200 on-demand and gpurentalprices A100_40GB on-demand (1.7 of the net 1.8 loss rise). 20 lines got worse, 8 better.
- Mechanism: a rate card that steps once, big, and then holds looks like a "run". One jump plus a small move in the same direction made a pace that was carried on linearly: medians up to +49% at h=7 (A100_40GB: last 1.99, median 3.26, truth 1.99).
- On Oct 1-7 dev origins 126 of 138 shifted medians went up, but only 63 were the right direction and 49 had no move at all. Prices stepped and held; they did not keep climbing.
- September made it look good: moves there were small up-creeps (mean 2%, 164 up / 94 down), so "runs go on" was true for a month.
- Why the keep rule passed it: the gate pooled skill over cells, so many tiny September wins hid a few huge losses. On these dev cells the gate falls 0.04, so it must have been judged when the gate held only September origins.
- A median cap tied to the line's largest past h-day move would not have fired: the jump itself set that bar.
- Lessons: (1) A median bet bigger than about two typical moves must pay for itself on its own cells, not hide in the pool. (2) Check the newest week of dev on its own; a regime change shows there first. (3) No single line may carry the gain or the loss.
- Do differently: never extrapolate a step line's jump; treat "one big step + hold" as a level change, not a trend. Port momentum only with a cap tied to the line's typical move size, not its largest.
- Guards G1-G3 (research/harness/guards.py, now in the keep rule) each reject this step, both as a keep and as a pick, and pass v7 and 19a5148.
